Engagement Overview

A practical, intensive 3-day Lead Auditor training programme designed to build real competence in ISO 27001:2022 and ISO 19011:2018 within the specific operational context of two African technology organisations.

Participating Organisations

  • Kropmann: Technology and cybersecurity solutions provider delivering cloud, infrastructure, automation, GRC and managed security services across Africa
  • Seamless Technologies: Workforce management SaaS platform processing sensitive HR and payroll data, expanding into AI and embedded finance

Training deliberately contextualised around their operational realities: multi-country delivery, cloud boundaries, privileged access management, AI/data flows, and regulatory compliance (NDPR, etc.)

Training Structure & Delivery Model
Element Details
Format Classroom (in-person), highly interactive
Duration 3 days × 6 hours = 18 contact hours
Group Size 4 participants (optimal for deep coaching and role-play)
Delivery Style 40% concept delivery + 60% practical application
Assessment Continuous (participation, exercises, role-plays, knowledge checks)
Outcome Focus Competence-based learning + formal examination preparation
Core Standards & Content Coverage
ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Clauses 4–10 (Context through Improvement). Risk-based thinking, PDCA cycle, Statement of Applicability, 93 Annex A controls (Organizational, People, Physical, Technological). Conformity vs. effectiveness.

ISO 19011:2018

Seven audit principles, managing an audit programme (Clause 5), conducting an audit (Clause 6), auditor competence (Clause 7). Stage 1 vs Stage 2 audits, evidence-based and risk-based auditing.

Lead Auditor Skills

Opening/closing meetings, interview technique, evidence gathering, writing clear nonconformities (Criteria–Finding–Evidence), leading an audit team, follow-up and corrective action verification.

Day-by-Day Curriculum

Day 1 – ISMS Foundations & Audit Perspective

Focus: Understanding ISO 27001 from an auditor's viewpoint

  • Structure of ISO 27001 and clause interpretation
  • ISMS scope definition exercise (multi-country, cloud boundaries)
  • Risk assessment methodology and treatment decisions
  • Statement of Applicability (SoA) development
  • Conformity vs. effectiveness in ISMS design
  • Case study: Defining ISMS scope for a SaaS platform with multi-cloud hosting

Day 2 – Audit Principles & Planning (ISO 19011)

Focus: Planning and preparing for effective audits

  • Seven principles of auditing applied in practice
  • Managing an audit programme (strategic overview)
  • Stage 1 audit: Document review and preliminary controls testing
  • Risk-based Stage 2 audit planning with realistic checklists
  • Auditor team composition and assignment
  • Exercise: Build a Stage 2 audit plan for a managed security services provider

Day 3 – Conducting Audits & Writing Findings

Focus: Practical execution and reporting (most hands-on day)

  • Three structured interview simulations with role rotation:
  • • Scenario 1: Access Control & Privileged Account Management (A.8.2, A.5.18)
  • • Scenario 2: Secure Development & AI Feature Release (A.8.25–A.8.32)
  • • Scenario 3: Incident Management & Customer Notification (A.5.24–A.5.28)
  • Writing clear nonconformities (Major vs. Minor)
  • Opening and closing meeting facilitation
  • Specimen audit report review with findings, risk scoring, executive summary
  • Corrective action tracking and follow-up verification
Use Cases & Master Case Study: "Nexus Africa Technologies"

Rather than generic examples, all training scenarios were built around a blended fictional organisation combining:

  • Multi-tenant SaaS Platform: Workforce/HR management (mirroring Seamless Technologies)
  • Cybersecurity & MSS: Managed security services delivery (mirroring Kropmann)
  • Multi-cloud Hosting: Azure and AWS shared responsibility model
  • Remote Delivery Teams: Distributed across African time zones
  • AI Features: External LLM integrations and data flow risks
  • Regulatory Context: NDPR, GDPR, local data residency requirements

Risk Matrix Applied in Training

Participants worked with a clear 5×5 risk scoring matrix used both for risk assessment exercises and audit finding prioritisation:

Score Range Risk Level Treatment Expectation
1–4 LOW Acceptable – monitor regularly
5–9 MEDIUM Treat within 90 days
10–14 HIGH Treat within 45 days – Management review
15–19 HIGH Treat within 30 days – CISO + MD attention
20–25 CRITICAL Treat within 7 days – CEO + Board attention
Key Learning: Risk scoring is not academic—it directly informs remediation timelines and stakeholder escalation. Auditors must understand business impact to credibly advise on risk treatment priorities.

Key Competencies Developed

By the end of Day 3, participants were competent to:

  • Interpret ISO 27001 requirements from an auditor's perspective
  • Apply ISO 19011 principles and processes in audit planning and execution
  • Plan a risk-based ISMS audit programme with realistic scope and sampling
  • Conduct effective interviews and gather objective evidence (not assumptions)
  • Write clear, factual, and actionable nonconformities (Criteria–Finding–Evidence format)
  • Distinguish between Major and Minor nonconformities based on risk and impact
  • Lead opening and closing meetings professionally
  • Link audit findings to business risk and regulatory obligations
  • Manage an audit team and assign roles effectively
  • Prepare for formal Lead Auditor certification examination with stronger practical grounding

Success Factors

  • Small group (4 people): Enabled deep coaching, meaningful role-play and individual feedback
  • Organisation-specific scenarios: Every exercise felt relevant to their real audit challenges
  • Progressive curriculum design: Knowledge → Planning → Execution (theory to practice pipeline)
  • Heavy emphasis on Day 3 practice: Role-plays, nonconformity writing exercises built real confidence before formal exams
  • Exam awareness woven throughout: Learning served both competence development and certification goals
  • Risk-based thinking reinforced: Every concept tied back to business impact and CISO priorities
Closing Thought

Effective Lead Auditor capability is not built by listening alone. It is built by practising—asking better questions, listening more carefully, demanding evidence, and writing findings that actually help the organisation improve.

This 3-day engagement gave the Kropmann and Seamless Technologies participants a structured, realistic environment to develop exactly those skills, using standards (ISO 27001:2022 and ISO 19011:2018) and scenarios that reflect the work they do every day.
